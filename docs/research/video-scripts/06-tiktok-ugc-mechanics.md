# 06. TikTok UGC mechanics and rules for a small tour operator

Scope: how TikTok UGC works, and the rules around it, for Outdoor Norway guides, staff and guests posting on their own accounts. Mechanics only. No company or brand pages were analyzed. Researched 2026-10-07.

Grades: A = platform official or peer reviewed. B = documented creator process or large dataset. C = my own small-sample measurement or practitioner lore. Legal points that do not come from a primary source are tagged [VERIFY with counsel]. They are tagged even when they do, because I am not a lawyer.

Read with 03 (platform facts, safe zones) and 05 (UK and US micro-creators). This note does not repeat them.

What this means for 25 s. Talking straight to camera runs 3.0 to 3.5 wps, or 75 to 88 words in 25 s. ### 1.3 UGC ad script conventions (practitioner, C)

UGC ad templates (admapix, sideshift, ugcsocial) agree on: hook, problem, product as the answer, one proof, CTA. About 35 to 40 words per 15 s. Cut 3 to 5 hook variants on one body. No brand in the first line, which matches TikTok's "branding subtle in the hook" (A). One proof signal only. Talking points, not word for word (C).

## 2. How UGC is structured, and how authenticity is described

TikTok's own words cover most of it. Lo-fi, 9:16, people on camera, "talking the lingo", text pop-ups, sound on, trends, a brief that leaves room for the creator's style (A, Creative Codes). The 7 Top Tips sheet adds that a phone is all you need, but that video must be well lit and 720p or better (A).

Official support exists for phone-shot lo-fi, good light, the creator's own voice, TikTok-style text and captions, and people on camera (A). Imperfect framing is practitioner lore only (C). Our rule: in-app font or a lookalike, never the brand typeface; natural light with readable faces; accents welcome.

UGC beat pattern for a guide-as-creator short:

1. Hook. A situation, a stake or a mistake, on frame one. Spoken and on screen.
2. Relatable moment. What the viewer already feels: cold hands, nerves on a first tour, "is it worth it".
3. Show the thing. The skin track, the snowshoe step, the hut, the sky. The camera does the work.
4. Proof. One fact from the claims bank, or one real guest reaction on camera.
5. Soft CTA. Save, send, comment. No booking ask in the spoken line.

## 3. Creator brief for guides and guests

TikTok says to give a clear brief and trust the creator (A). A UGC brief is not a script. It fixes what must be true and leaves how it is said to the person.

Brief fields the skill should output:

| Field | Content | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Format | One of the 05 formats (tip, myth-bust, POV day, gear check, guest reaction, "things I wish I knew") | 1 |
| The one message | What the viewer should remember | 12 words |
| Hook options | 3 alternatives, each 6 to 11 words, first person or "you" | 3 |
| Talking points | Bullets, not sentences | 3 |
| Must-say facts | From the claims bank, with source id | 1 to 2, max 3 |
| Must-not-say | Guarantees on snow or aurora, scarcity, superlatives, "send it", "no avalanche risk", difficulty put-downs, price unless approved | list |
| Disclosure | Spoken and on-screen line, see 4.1 | required |
| Shot list | 4 to 6 shots: phone position (selfie, chest POV, tripod in snow), action, length in s | 6 |
| Safety | Gear visible on touring shots, no guest in hazard, guide in position | checklist |
| Consent | Who is identifiable, and the release status of each | per person |
| Length | Target 15 to 22 s, hard max 25 s | |
| Sound | Original voice. Music only from CML if the account is business or the post is commercial | |
| Text | Max 2 lines, max 6 words per line, inside safe zone | |
| CTA | One soft CTA, 8 words or fewer | 1 |
| Caption and pinned comment | Booking info and link pointer live here | |
| Do and don't | Do: film extra, start mid-action, say it your way. Don't: read from a phone, greet, add logos or stock music | |

## 4. Commercial and legal rules

### 4.1 Disclosure

| Rule | What it says | Source | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok toggle, own business | "Disclosure is required when you're: Promoting your own business, product, or service". Undisclosed content may get reduced visibility; repeated failure can bring posting limits or a ban | TikTok Community Guidelines, Commercial Disclosure and Paid Marketing, released 25 Aug 2026, effective 24 Sep 2026 | A |
| TikTok toggle, third party | Branded content promotes a third-party brand for any incentive: gifts, paid posts, affiliate, ambassador. Product must be clear from the video or caption | TikTok Branded Content Policy, published 4 Aug 2026, effective 31 Aug 2026 | A |
| Travel not restricted | Travel is not on the prohibited or restricted lists | same | A |
| Toggle labels | "Your brand" gives "Promotional content". "Branded content" gives "Paid partnership". Label cannot be changed after posting | Third-party guides (hireinfluence, emplicit) | B [VERIFY in app] |
| Norway, who labels | Anyone paid or given a benefit (free trip, discount, product) to post. No written agreement needed. Advertiser has main responsibility and must train and monitor | Forbrukertilsynet, Veiledning om merking av reklame i sosiale medier (2022, updated 29 Apr 2025) | A [VERIFY with counsel] |
| Norway, words | "reklame" or "annonse" are always clear enough. In English use "advertisement" or "advertising". Advises against "ad", "sponset", "i samarbeid med", "ambassadør". Platform "paid partnership" label alone is not enough | same | A [VERIFY with counsel] |
| Norway, placement | Label must catch the eye at once, come first in the text. On TikTok and Reels the video itself should be labelled too | same | A [VERIFY with counsel] |
| US, FTC | Disclose in the video, not only the description. Early, not at the end. Spoken and on screen together works best. Employees "should disclose your relationship". Profile listing "isn't enough". "#ad" or "Ad:" at the start likely effective | FTC, Endorsement Guides: What People Are Asking | A |
| UK, ASA | "#ad" upfront and prominent is the clearest label. Platform tools may be used in addition or instead | ASA, via IAB UK and ASA news | B [VERIFY CAP Code] |
| EU, posing as a consumer | Falsely representing oneself as a consumer is a blacklisted practice (UCPD Annex I, point 22). Commission Notice 2021/C 526/01 covers influencers | EUR-Lex, via Osborne Clarke and others | B [VERIFY with counsel] |

What this means for us. Outdoor Norway is a Norwegian trader, so Norwegian law applies to its marketing whatever the audience [VERIFY with counsel]. That makes the strictest set the working rule:

- Staff and guide posts about the company: TikTok toggle on ("Your brand" if posted for the company, "Branded content" if a guide posts about their employer [VERIFY which applies]). Plus a spoken relationship line in the first 3 to 5 s ("I guide for Outdoor Norway") and an on-screen and first-line caption label "Advertisement".
- Guests given anything (discount, upgrade, gear) or asked to post: advertising, label as above. Unprompted posts after a paid trip are not, until we boost them (4.3).
- Never let staff appear as ordinary guests. No "I booked this trip" from a guide.
- "#ad" alone passes in the UK and US but is discouraged in Norway. Use "Advertisement".

### 4.2 Music

| Rule | Source | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| CML: "a pre-cleared global music library of 1 million songs". "Businesses cannot use the general music library for commercial usage". Covers organic, ads, branded content. Other users' sounds: "consult your legal team" | TikTok Ads Help, Commercial Music Library | A |
| Business accounts see only CML in the picker | soundstripe, planoly | B |
| CML clearance covers TikTok only, not Reels or Shorts | third-party guides | C [VERIFY with counsel] |

Rule for us. A guide's personal account posting about the company is commercial content, so trending chart audio is out even on a personal account [VERIFY with counsel]. Use the creator's own voice, natural sound (skins, wind, boots in snow) and CML tracks. Natural sound is the most native option and has no licence risk. When the same file goes to Reels and Shorts, strip CML music and re-add music cleared for that platform.

### 4.3 Spark Ads and reuse

Spark Ads run organic posts as ads (TikTok Ads Help, A). The creator turns on ad authorization per video and sends a code with a customizable duration (A); guides say 7 to 365 days, default 30 (B). Duets and stitches need both creators' codes, and the caption locks once authorized (B [VERIFY]).

Rules for us. Ask for codes of 60 days or more. Get a written reuse release before boosting a guest post, and make sure it already carries the label, since the caption locks. Agree in writing what happens to authorized posts when a guide leaves [VERIFY with counsel].

### 4.4 Guests on camera

Norway: publishing an image where a person is the main subject needs their consent (Åndsverkloven § 104), with an exception for situation images of public events. Identifiable people are personal data under GDPR, so consent must be informed and documentable. Minors need guardian consent (all B, secondary summaries of Lovdata, Datatilsynet and Helsedirektoratet [VERIFY with counsel]).

Rules for us. Written release before filming a guest as the subject. Separate tick boxes for organic posting, staff personal accounts, paid ads and other platforms. Right to withdraw, with a named contact. A small group in the background of a wide shot is lower risk but still personal data, so ask the group at the trip briefing and offer a "no camera" sticker or band [VERIFY with counsel].

### 4.5 Safety on camera

TikTok Community Guidelines, 2026 H2 (A): risky activity by non-professionals that could be imitated and cause significant harm is removed; moderate-harm content is FYF ineligible and 18+. "Showing professionals engaging in combat sports, extreme sports, or stunts" is allowed, with warning labels. Reused clips with other watermarks are FYF ineligible.

Rules for us. Guides are the professionals, so guide-led touring and off-piste is allowed, but it may get a warning label. Frame it as guided practice. Show beacons, shovels and probes, the guide in position, spacing on slopes. Never show a guest doing something unguided in avalanche terrain. Never show cornices, cliff drops or jumps as something to copy. No "send it" language. Do not repost other creators' clips.

## 5. Calls to action that do not sound like an ad

Soft CTAs that fit UGC (all C): "Save this for your Norway trip" (saves, for tips and packing). "Send this to your ski touring friend" (sends, for group trips; sends are a named Instagram signal, B, see 03). "What would you pack?" (comments). "Part 2 tomorrow: the hut" (series return). "Link in bio" belongs in the caption, not the spoken line.

Rules:

- One CTA only, 8 words or fewer, last 4 s. Same as now.
- Comment prompts must be real questions. "Comment YES if..." for reach and "like for part 2" are engagement bait. TikTok makes "Like-for-like promises" and "False incentives" FYF ineligible (A).
- "Comment TOUR and I'll send the link" is a keyword prompt. It is not named in TikTok's rules, but it trades a comment for a reward. Treat it as a risk. Do not use it in organic UGC (C).
- Link in bio needs a business account or about 1,000 followers on a personal account, and varies by region (B, third-party). Guides with small accounts should say "details in the caption" and put the URL as text, or tag the company account.
- Booking and price go in caption or pinned comment, not the spoken line.

## 6. Where authenticity clashes with brand rules

| Authentic cue | Current brand rule | Conflict | How to keep both |
|---|---|---|---|
| First person hook ("I nearly quit on day one") | Hook must address "you" | Real | Allow I, my, we when the hook names a situation, stake or mistake. Keep "you" in the body |
| Name intro ("I'm Kari, a guide in Voss") | Greetings blocked | Partial | Allow a name and role intro of 8 words or fewer. Keep "hey guys", "welcome back" blocked |
| Casual words (honestly, okay so, kinda) | Clean voiceover style | Mild | Allow up to 3 per short. Never script um or uh |
| Humor and self-deprecation | No negative framing of difficulty | Real | Joke about the guide or the weather, never about the tour being hard or the guest failing. "My legs were jelly" is fine. "This is brutal" is not |
| Slang ("stoked", "pow") | Cliché list | Mild | Allow ski slang the audience uses. Block marketing clichés ("winter wonderland", "hidden gem", "bucket list") |
| Hype ("best day ever") | No unsupported superlatives | Real | Allow as clearly personal feeling only when tied to "my" and a moment ("my best day this season"). Never about the product ("the best tour in Norway") |
| Aurora excitement | No aurora guarantees | Real | Show it when it happened, with the date. Say "we got lucky tonight". Never "you will see" |
| Fresh snow hype | No snow guarantees | Real | Date the clip. "This was Tuesday" not "every day is like this" |
| No logo | Brand recall | Real | Brand appears through the disclosure line, the guide's jacket if worn naturally, and the caption tag. No end card |

## 8. Changes the validator and skill need for UGC style

These build on 05, section 8.

1. `style: ugc | voiceover`. Default voiceover. All rules below apply only to ugc.
2. Pace allowance, facts unchanged. Short ugc spoken: target 55 to 65 words, hard max 70 in 25 s (2.8 effective wps). Per-beat speaking rate may reach 3.5 wps. Keep 1 fact per beat, max 3 per script, recommend 2. Reason: measured UGC-length talking runs 3.46 wps median (C, n = 6), but our footage needs silent action gaps.
3. Hook. First spoken word or first overlay at 0.0 to 0.5 s. 6 to 11 words inside 3 s (was 4 to 9). Reason: short UGC clips carried 8 to 11 words in the first 3 s (C), and TikTok says land the proposition in 3 s (A).
4. First person allowed in the hook if it names a situation, stake or mistake. "You" stays preferred in the body. A name and role intro of 8 words or fewer passes. Greeting blocklist unchanged.
5. Disclosure beat required. Role `disclose`, inside the first 5 s, spoken relationship line plus overlay containing "Advertisement" (or "Reklame" in Norwegian content). Caption must start with the same word. Plus a `tiktok_toggle: your_brand | branded_content` field. Reason: Forbrukertilsynet wants the label in the video and first in text (A [VERIFY with counsel]); FTC wants it early, spoken and on screen (A); TikTok requires the toggle for own-business promotion (A). The disclose beat does not count toward hook words or CTA.
6. Block "#ad" as the only label, and block "sponsored", "in collaboration with", "ambassador" as labels. Reason: Norwegian guidance (A [VERIFY with counsel]).
7. No end cards, logos, title slates, brand stings or brand fonts in ugc. Fail any beat with role `endcard`, `logo` or `title`.
8. Native text. Max 2 lines on screen, 6 words and 32 characters per line (unchanged). Lowercase allowed. Each overlay visible for at least words / 5 s and at least 1.2 s. Max 4 overlays per short.
9. CTA. Exactly 1, 8 words or fewer, last 4 s, verb from Save, Send, Comment, Follow, Ask. Block "Book" and price in the spoken line for ugc. Block engagement bait: "like for", "comment YES", "follow for part 2 if", keyword-for-link prompts. Booking line goes to `caption` and `pinned_comment`.
10. Sound field. `sound: original | natural | cml`. Fail `trending` for any commercial post. Add a `repost_platforms` list that triggers a music re-clear warning.
11. Casual markers. Allow okay so, honestly, kinda, like, wait. Max 3 per short. Never um or uh in a script.
12. Brand rules adapted, not dropped. Superlatives allowed only as dated personal feeling with "my" ("my best day this season"). Snow and aurora lines require a date or "this was" framing. Difficulty humor allowed only about the guide or weather.
13. Safety lint. Block "send it", "full send", "no risk", "totally safe", "anyone can do this off-piste". For any role `show` in avalanche terrain, require `safety_shot: gear_visible | guide_in_position | spacing`. TikTok warns or limits extreme-sport clips (A), so frame as guided.
14. Consent field per identifiable person: `guide | staff | guest_release_organic | guest_release_paid | none`. `none` fails if identifiable. Minors need `guardian_release`. Spark Ads use needs `guest_release_paid`.
15. No staff-as-guest. Fail if a staff creator uses booking-customer phrasing ("I booked", "we paid for"). Reason: UCPD Annex I point 22 (B [VERIFY with counsel]).
16. Creator brief output, next to the beat table, with the section 3 fields. Mark spoken lines as talking points by default. Only the hook options, disclosure line and CTA are word-for-word.
17. Guest proof lines are never scripted. Role `proof` with `speaker: guest` must be `unscripted: true`. The skill writes a prompt question for the guide to ask, not the answer.
18. Long ugc (3:20 max): keep 05's 520 words at 2.6 wps, disclosure in the first 10 s.
