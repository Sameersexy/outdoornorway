## Caption
7 days: ski, maybe northern lights.
This is the 7 Day Ski in Fjord Norway & Aurora in Tromsø, from Bergen. Here is the trip day by day.

Day 1: Your adventure begins in Bergen. A guide is with you for the whole trip.
Day 2: Morning train from Bergen to Voss, about an hour. Then a guided snowshoe tour.
Day 3: One ski day at Voss Resort. A one-day ski pass is included. You can also rent skis, boots, poles and helmets.
Day 4: Flight from Bergen to Tromsø. The flight is included.
Day 5: Fjord trip on a private boat. In the evening at the hotel camp, look for the northern lights, if the sky is clear.
Day 6: Dog sledding. Then a night trip to look for the northern lights. They may not appear.
Day 7: We take you to Tromsø Airport. The time fits your flight.

Included: a guide for the whole trip, a 1-day ski pass at Voss Resort and the flight from Bergen to Tromsø.
The price does not include flights to or from Norway.
Nobody can promise the northern lights. They may or may not show.

From NOK 41,181 (NOK is Norwegian kroner). Start dates in 2027:

6 Feb to 12 Feb 2027
