## Caption
When should you go to see the northern lights in Norway? In Northern Norway the viewing window is roughly September through March, and exact dates shift with latitude. September and October have dark nights without deep winter conditions. November through January has the longest darkness. February and March have dark evenings and more daylight. No single month settles it. Clear skies and solar activity matter more than the calendar month, and no timing can promise a sighting. Part two, on how to go, comes next.
