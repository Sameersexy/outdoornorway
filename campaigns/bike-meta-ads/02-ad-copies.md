# Ad copies: two bike tours, six single image Meta ads

Paste-ready copy for six Meta feed ads (4:5 image, three lines on the image). Written with the adquill skill and checked against the two tour pages on 2026-10-12. The full file with receipts, group chat lines, photo direction, the policy and fact pass and the open items is `01-single-image-ads.md`.

Status:

- Tour A (A1 to A3) is a draft. The live 9 day page shows no price, so these ads carry none. Do not publish until the owner confirms the price and what visitors see on the page.
- Tour B (B1 to B3) is ready to paste after two landing page checks: the page badge "All equipment + guide" contradicts the ad line "Bike rental is extra", and the per-person basis of "From NOK 40,167" is unconfirmed.

How to use: the three image lines go on the photo, added by a designer or a layout tool and never by an image generator. The primary text, headline, description and CTA button go in Ads Manager. The reels line is for Reels and Stories placements. Switch off every Advantage+ text option for these ads, because a rewritten price or date is a trust problem [VERIFY: reported by third parties only, no Meta page read].
