# Meta single image ads: two bike tours (6 ads)

Brief: six single image, glanceable Meta feed ads (4:5), three per tour, written with the adquill skill (`adquill/skills/adquill/SKILL.md`). Pages read 2026-10-12:

- Tour A: 9-Day Premium e-MTB Adventure, https://www.outdoornorway.com/9-day-bike-tour-norway
- Tour B: 7 Day Mountain Bike Tour in Norway, https://www.outdoornorway.com/multi-day-tour/mountain-bike-tour-in-norway

Status:

- Tour A (A1 to A3): draft, not ready to publish. The live page shows no price (the price slot reads "Loading..."), so these ads carry none. Confirm what visitors see on the page first.
- Tour B (B1 to B3): ready to paste after the two landing page checks in "Open items", items 2 and 3.

Platform data verified 2026-10-06 (adquill). Both tours are summer tours, so every date is 2027 and no 2026 date is used.

## Diagnosis

- Awareness level: problem-aware (defaulted, not checked against competitor ads).
- Sophistication stage: 3 (guessed, the Meta Ad Library was not checked).
- Job to be done: pick a guided multi-day bike trip in Norway without planning the route, shuttles and stays.
- Main anxiety: Tour A, the entry level and what the money covers. Tour B, what the price covers (the bike is not in it) and how much of the riding is walking.

## Image spec brief (for the designer)

- Ratio and size: 4:5, 1440 x 1800 px, JPG or PNG, under 30 MB (Meta Ads Guide, verified 2026-10-06).
- Margins: keep all text at least 5 percent from every edge (72 px at the sides, 90 px at top and bottom). adquill heuristic, not a Meta rule.
- Type hierarchy, Tour B: price (line 3) heaviest, dates (line 2) second, name (line 1) third. Tour A has no price, so use the same weight order with the line 3 fact in the middle weight and the name heaviest. Starting sizes to test: 160 px, 90 px, 80 px. Adjust until all three lines read at 375 px wide.
- Contrast: text on a plain area or a solid band, at least 4.5 to 1.
- Image lines: use the three image lines of each ad as written. Line 2 is the list of start dates. If there is room, the designer may add the word "Starts" in a smaller size above it.
- Photos: real photos of the real tours, taken with consent. No stock, no generated people, no third-party logos (keep bike brand logos out of close-ups).
- 9:16 version, only if the ad runs in Stories and Reels: 1080 x 1920 px. Keep text out of the top 269 px, the bottom 672 px and 65 px at each side. The reels_line goes in this version (Meta Reels spec, verified 2026-10-06).
- Text is added in a layout tool or by a designer. An image generator never renders it.

## Policy and fact pass

Block: none.

Warn:
- A1 "Our tour suits intermediate to advanced riders" and A3 headline "Intermediate to advanced" are skill labels about the tour. Skill is not a listed Meta personal attribute. Keep them in the third person and never turn them into a question to the viewer.
- B3 "dietary restrictions" stays in the third person for the same reason.

Note:
- Tour B price is "From NOK 40,167" in every slot, as on the page. The per-person basis is not stated, so no ad says "per person".
- B1 to B3 say "Bike rental is extra". The Tour B page badge reads "All equipment + guide", which contradicts it. Fix the page before launch (Open items, item 3).
- Tour A image_line_3 holds a substitute fact because there is no price. A cold, high-ticket ad is normally tested with and without a price, so test again once the price is confirmed.
- Image_line_2 lists start dates only. Tour A trips start on a Saturday and end on a Sunday, Tour B trips start on a Friday and the page gives no end dates.

Fact check: every number maps to a page fact. Tour A: 9 days, 6 riding days, 5 Jun, 26 Jun, 21 Aug 2027, 360 degrees, 4 to 7 hours, 3 starts. Tour B: 7 days, 6 Aug, 20 Aug, 3 Sep 2027, NOK 40,167, day 6, days 2, 3 and 4, 90 minutes, day 4. Weekdays checked by calendar calculation. No testimonial, rating, award, certification, seat count or deadline appears.

## Review trail

Eighteen draft ads (nine angles per tour) were written to the adquill slot budgets and checked by a script (character counts, the adquill linter, numbers against the ledger, banned words, repeated sentences). Three reviewer personas from `adquill/agents/` (direct-response, editorial, creative) then reviewed all 18 independently. Their picks were merged with the panel rule from `adquill/skills/adquill/assets/panel-prompt.md`: facts and policy go to direct-response, language to editorial, concept to creative. The six strongest were revised, reviewed once more by the same three personas, and adjusted once more. Two small edits (the B2 reels line and the B3 primary text) were made after the last read, because each said slightly more than the page does.

Panel verdicts after round 2 (before the last adjustment pass): A1 ship, revise, revise. A2 ship, revise, revise. A3 revise, revise, revise. B1 ship, revise, revise. B2 ship, ship, revise. B3 revise, revise, revise (direct-response, editorial, creative). No ad had three ship verdicts. The last adjustment pass was checked by the script and a full read against the page, not by a third panel round. Test the six with real spend before calling any of them a winner.
