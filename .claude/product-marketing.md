## Who we are

Outdoor Norway runs guided outdoor tours from Voss, in Western Norway. Winter is skiing, ski touring, snowshoeing and, on one trip, huskies and northern lights in Tromsø.

- Mission: create outdoor adventures that leave guests empowered, inspired and connected to the natural world.
- Values: sustainability, local expertise, guest empowerment.

## Audience (seven segments)

1. Alps-weary intermediate and advanced skiers who want variety and no queues (9 Day).
2. First-time Norway visitors who want fjords and the Arctic in one trip, aurora as a bonus (7 Day).
3. Adults learning to ski or skiing occasionally, who fear holding the group up (6 Day).
4. Families and friend groups wanting a short winter break with ski and non-ski days (3-Day Ski and Snowshoe).
5. Couples and remote workers who need a long weekend that fits a working week (3-Day Multi-Sport, Valentine's departure).
6. Confident resort skiers curious about ski touring (Backcountry Beginner to Intermediate).
7. Experienced off-piste skiers who want real terrain and local knowledge (Backcountry Intermediate to Advanced).

## Voice (from the client's brand voice rules)

- Warm, confident, friendly. Not pushy, not robotic, not overly casual.
- Descriptive and sensory, nature-inspired. Not generic, cliched or exaggerated.
- Short and vivid lines, mixed with one calmer flowing line.
- Speak to "you". Use "we" for what the team looks after. Never "Outdoor Norway offers" or "guests will".
- Lead with what the guest gets, not the itinerary.
- Describe difficulty by what the day involves ("long ascents", "confident on skis"), never with warnings or "only for experts".
- Numerals for numbers. Dates in full with the year: "30 Jan to 7 Feb 2027".
- House style: no em or en dashes, no emojis, no capital letters for emphasis, short sentences, no filler transitions.

Words to use: fjord, gondola, chairlift, guide, small group, skins, snowshoe, frozen waterfall, campfire, waffle, mountain cafe, train from Bergen (about an hour), husky team, aurora (as a possibility).
Words to avoid: winter wonderland, breathtaking, magical, unforgettable, epic, stunning, bucket list, hidden gem, journey, adrenaline, perfect powder, pristine, seamless, hassle-free, best, most scenic (unless "the most scenic trip we run").

## Do not claim

- "6 days of skiing" on the 6 Day package. The page says three ski days and calls it "a mixed winter holiday, not a six-day ski trip".
- "Ski days" in the plural on the 7 Day trip. It has one ski day.
- Any aurora sighting or guarantee. The page says no departure can guarantee one.
- "Guaranteed snow", "perfect powder", "all gear included" (contradicted on several pages), any group size number, any age limit (pages conflict).
- "Few slots left", countdowns or any scarcity wording, unless live availability is confirmed. Real departure dates are fine.
- "Per person" until confirmed. Use "from NOK X". Day tours say "per adult".
- A rating badge without a named source and current number ("Tripadvisor 4.9", not "4.9 stars") [VERIFY].
- Beginner positioning on the 3-Day Ski and Snowshoe or the 3-Day Multi-Sport. The pages contradict themselves on level [VERIFY].
- Personal-attribute questions to the viewer ("Are you a beginner?", "Over 60?"). Describe the trip instead.

## Channel notes

- Meta feed static: 4:5 image (1440 x 1800), about 125 visible characters of primary text, Facebook headline about 27 characters, Instagram feed may hide the headline. The image must carry name, dates and price.
- The three ads that the client audit found generate more leads share: a named product, a human-scale photo, short rhythmic copy. The strongest layout is name, a date line, and a price pill ("STARTING FROM NOK 14,165").
- Turn off Advantage+ text edits on price and date ads. Use Meta UTMs, not `utm_source=chatgpt.com`.
