## The one message

Slide the ski, don't lift it, and you keep your legs for the ski down.

## Approved facts

Say these, and nothing else that sounds like a fact.

| id | Fact | Source |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Ingrid guides ski tours for Brekke Ski Guides | fictional: Brekke guide list |
| F2 | Lifting the ski on each step means carrying the ski and skin up the whole climb | fictional: Brekke guide training card |
| F3 | Brekke guides teach a sliding step: the ski stays on the snow and glides forward, which saves the legs on long climbs | fictional: Brekke guide training card |

## Do not say

- Anything about the snow being good every day, or "you'll always get powder". If the snow was great, say the day: "this was Tuesday".
- "Brutal", "leg killer", "only for experts", "not for beginners". Joke about yourself, never about the guest struggling.
- "Best", "the only", "world-class" about Brekke or the place.
- "Spots are going fast" or any scarcity line.
- "Hey guys", "welcome back", "that's it".
- "Send it", "no risk", "totally safe".
- Never "I booked this trip". You work here.

## Hook

Main: "I watch almost every new guest do this on the climb."
Alternate 1: "If your legs are gone halfway up, look at your feet." (expectation flip, 11 words)
Alternate 2: "Someone asked me why touring wore them out. It's this." (someone asked me, 10 words)

Start talking at frame one, already walking. No hello first.

Film every beat two or three times. Phone vertical, mic near your mouth, light on your face.
