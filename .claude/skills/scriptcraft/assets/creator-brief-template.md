## Do not say

From `scripts/brand_rules.json`. The validator blocks these in the script; on the day, they are on you.

- Guarantees about snow, powder, weather, wildlife or the aurora: "guaranteed", "always", "for sure", "definitely", "every morning", "never miss", "100 percent". Say what was there, with the date.
- Scarcity: "last chance", "spots are going fast", "filling up fast", "limited spots", "hurry", "don't miss out".
- Hype and cliche: "epic", "breathtaking", "magical", "stunning", "unforgettable", "winter wonderland", "bucket list", "hidden gem", "perfect powder", "once in a lifetime".
- Superlatives without a source: "best", "biggest", "ultimate", "world-class", "number one", "the only".
- Negative framing of difficulty: "brutal", "extreme", "only for experts", "not for beginners", "you'll suffer", "leg killer".
- Greetings and sign-offs: "hey guys", "welcome back", "in this video", "that's it", "like and subscribe".
- Company-speak and sales verbs: "our guests", "guests will", "we offer", "book today", "secure your spot".
- Safety: "send it", "full send", "no risk", "totally safe".
- Never say "I booked this trip" if you work for us.
