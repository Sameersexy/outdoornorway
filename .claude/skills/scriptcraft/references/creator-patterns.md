# Creator patterns: what repeats, what transfers

A compact summary of the creators and brands studied for this skill (research dated 2026-10-07). Measured numbers come from a small caption sample (19 videos, YouTube auto captions) and are grade C. Almost no creator publishes words per second or facts per minute; do not invent them.

## Who was studied

| Creator or brand | What they do that matters here | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| MrBeast (production memo) | Minute 1 meets the thumbnail promise. "Stop telling people what they will be watching and start showing them." Re-engagement at about 3 and 6 min of a 13 min video. Abrupt end after the payoff | B |
| Veritasium | Opens on the common misconception, makes the viewer commit, then shows the result | A research, B practice |
| Kurzgesagt | About a dozen script drafts "to cut away unnecessary bits". Public source sheet and expert review per video | B |
| George Blackman and Ali Abdaal | Title and hook first; hook "should only contain critical information"; set up, tension, payoff every 1 to 2 min | B |
| Mario Joos (ex MrBeast editor) | Novelty matters more than cut speed. "Structural context is god-like" | C |
| Nas Daily | One story per minute, big subtitles, fixed sign-off. Measured 2.34 to 2.57 words per second | B, C |
| Drew Binsky | Moved from "what's up guys, welcome to" (2019) to claim-first openers (2026) | C |
| Kara and Nate | Journey as an obstacle, an honest downside before the verdict, a question plus share CTA | C |
| Lost LeBlanc | Price loop opened at 10 s, paid at 101 s of 111 s. "By day... later that night" day frame | C |
| Mark Wiens | Sensory promise plus place in the hook | C |
| Visit Norway, winter family film | Third person, five characters with one want each, activities as a verb list | C |
| Inspired by Iceland, Ask Gudmundur | Locals answer real viewer questions | B (platform case study) |
| Visit Finland, Rent a Finn | Sells a person and a feeling, names Norway's fjords-and-lights list as the category cliche | B (agency case) |
| Hank Green | Paused channels in August 2026 after relying "too much on generated notes". The cost of unverified AI research | C, cautionary |

Measured pace: creator voiceover median 2.75 words per second (shorts 2.87, long 2.55). Narrated brand films 1.35 to 1.82. Every creator short starts speaking by 0.4 s. Only 1 of 9 sampled creator shorts fits under 25 s.

## Patterns that repeat

| Pattern | Who | How scriptcraft uses it |
|---|---|---|
| Promise written before the script | MrBeast, Abdaal, Veritasium | `title` and `core_takeaway` come first; long promise by 30 s |
| First seconds confirm the promise, no greeting | MrBeast, Binsky 2026, every short sampled | Hook rules H01, H02, BR01 |
| Show, then explain | MrBeast, Johnny Harris | Visual column first; the voice adds what the picture cannot |
| One question, paid off in steps | Blackman, MrBeast, Nas Daily | Chapters with mini-payoffs, partial answer, L01 |
| Escalation, best shot last | MrBeast | Strongest shot on the payoff beat |
| Simplicity is policed | Kurzgesagt drafts, MrBeast rules | Word, fact and sentence budgets in code |
| Short endings | MrBeast, Nas Daily | One CTA, no sign-off, BR02 |
| Sources shown | Kurzgesagt, Veritasium | Facts table, F04 to F07, [VERIFY] |
| Honest downside before the verdict | Kara and Nate | One plain limit in long scripts |
| A day as scenes, not dates | Lost LeBlanc, Visit Norway | "By the climb... at the top..." instead of "Day 1, Day 2" |

## What does not transfer

| Practice | Why not | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| Spectacle re-engagements | Needs money, stunts, crew | A place reveal, a guide on camera, a sound break |
| 37 to 122 s creator shorts | Our cap is 25 s | Copy the hook and close, cut the middle |
| 10 to 20 min explainers | Our cap is 200 s | 4 to 6 chapters of 25 to 50 s |
| 3 to 3.8 words per second | Breaks the calm guide voice and overloads | Budget at 2.4 (short) and 2.2 (long) |
| First person host ("I tried") | No recurring face | "You" and "we", or a named guide as narrator |
| Fear hooks and superlatives | Client rules ban them | Curiosity and specifics |
| Clickbait the video does not pay | YouTube intro metric punishes mismatch | "Legit bait": enticing and accurate |
| Signature sign-offs | Cost 3 to 5 s of a 25 s short | One-line CTA, the end card does the rest |
| Comment-keyword CTAs | Need DM automation [VERIFY: does Outdoor Norway run one?] | Send, save or book |
