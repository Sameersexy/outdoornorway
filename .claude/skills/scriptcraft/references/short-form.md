# Short form playbook: 25 seconds

For Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Hard max 25 s, warn above 23 s, 60 words hard max, 40 to 55 target. Limits in tiers.md.

A 25 s short carries one idea. Sampled travel creators publish 37 to 122 s shorts; our cap is 2 to 5 times tighter. Copy their hook and close, never their middle.

## Beat template

| Beat | Time | Words | Role | Job |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-3 | 4 to 7 | hook, open:A | Motion on screen, "you" in the line, the one question opened |
| 2 | 3-7 | 8 to 10 | setup | The guest's situation, in a picture |
| 3 | 7-12 | 8 to 12 | build | The detail that makes the question sharper. One fact at most |
| 4 | 12-17 | 8 to 12 | shift or explain | The turn: the thing the viewer did not expect |
| 5 | 17-21 | 8 to 9 | payoff, close:A | The answer. The strongest shot |
| 6 | 21-25 | 8 max | cta | One ask, starting in the last 4 s |

Total about 50 words, estimated 21 to 23 s. Five beats also work: merge 3 and 4.

Why 7 words in the hook when the tier allows 9: at 2.4 words per second, 8 or 9 words need 3.3 to 3.8 s and trigger a T04 warning in a 3 s beat. 10 words block (H01). Keep the hook to what fits.

## Hook formulas

Each must be paid off by beat 5. A hook the video does not answer trains people to scroll.

| Formula | Pattern | Example (illustrative) |
|---|---|---|
| Surprising rule | Your guide wants [unexpected thing]. | "Your guide wants you a little cold." |
| Outcome first | Show the end, then ask how. | "You skied down that. Here's the climb." |
| Specific question | A question about the trip, never about the viewer. | "How do skis go uphill?" |
| Small scene | Time, place, one sense. | "Seven a.m. Just your skins on the snow." |
| Counter-belief | Name a common belief, then test it. Frame as discovery, never "you are wrong". | "Touring looks steep. Watch the guide's pace." |

Banned openers: greetings, "in this video", "welcome to", the brand name, a rhetorical "Ready to...?", personal-attribute questions ("Are you a beginner?"). BR01 and BR08 enforce most of them.

## Visual hooks for beat 1

Frame 1 is already moving. One subject, high contrast, readable with the sound off.

- POV skis on skins sliding uphill, close.
- A skier drops into the slope, snow spraying.
- A guide turns to camera mid-climb and points.
- Hands clip a binding, then the cut to the slope.
- A drone pulls back from one small figure on a white ridge.

Never: a logo, a title card, black, a fade in, a talking head saying hello (H02 blocks these).

## Loop-back ending

A short is watched more than once. If the last line runs into the first, the replay feels continuous.

- Last line: "...so you start the day cold." First line: "Your guide wants you a little cold."
- Choose one per short: a spoken CTA or a spoken loop-back. If you loop, put the CTA in the overlay and caption only, and make the final spoken beat still carry the cta role with at most 8 words.

## Overlays and captions

- Max 6 words, 32 characters per line, 2 lines. On screen at least 1.5 s.
- Beat 1 always has an overlay: most feed video starts muted.
- An overlay adds a keyword or names the question. It never repeats the voice (O03 warns above 60 percent overlap).
- Burned-in captions are a separate track for sound-off viewers. They are not overlays and do not go in the text column.
- Details in visuals-and-text.md.

## Call to action

- Exactly one. Starts in the last 4 s. Max 8 words.
- One verb: send, save, follow, comment, ask, book.
- "Send this to..." targets sends per reach, a signal Instagram names as important (B).
- "Book" only when the overlay or caption carries the real date and price from the facts table.
- Never stack asks ("like, follow and book").

## Weak versus strong

| Weak | Why it fails | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| "Hey guys, welcome to Voss!" | Greeting spends the 3 s window | "Your guide wants you a little cold." |
| "Fjords, mountains, snow and amazing views." | Noun list, no stake, no picture | "The fjord sits below your ski tips." |
| "Join us for 9 days, 4 resorts, from NOK 42,126!" | 3 numbers in one breath | Trip in the voice, "From NOK 42,126" alone on the CTA overlay |
| "Experience perfect powder on our epic tours." | BR03 words, a snow promise | "The guide picks the slope from this week's snow." |
| "Only for experienced skiers." | Negative framing (BR05) | "For skiers confident on red runs." |
| "Book now, limited spots!" | Scarcity (BR04) | "Day tours every Saturday and Sunday. Link below." |
| Overlay: "Your guide wants you cold" over the same spoken line | Redundant | Overlay: "Why start cold?" |

## Checklist before running the validator

- [ ] One idea. Can you say it in one sentence of under 12 words?
- [ ] Beat 1: motion, "you", overlay, 7 words or fewer.
- [ ] Every number is in the facts table with verified yes.
- [ ] At most 3 facts, 2 numbers, 1 place name.
- [ ] Payoff closes the hook question in the last or second-last beat.
- [ ] One CTA, last 4 s, 8 words or fewer.
- [ ] Every beat has 2 shots unless it is 3 s or shorter.
