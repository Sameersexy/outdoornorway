Typical day: meet 08:30, skins on 09:15, first descent about 11:40, back at the car about 15:30. Six per guide.

The guide picks the route the evening before, from the forecast. If the day is called off for avalanche risk, you pick another Saturday or get a full refund.

Gear hire is NOK 650 extra. Skis, boots and lunch are yours to bring.
