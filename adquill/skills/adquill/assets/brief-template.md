# Brief template

A fill-in brief for adquill. Read it at step 1 of the method, copy the form, fill what you know and leave the rest blank. adquill asks about every blank required field before it writes.

## How to fill it

Write facts, not feelings. "Six people per guide, 08:30 start" helps. "Small friendly groups" does not. If you do not know a fact, write "unknown". An honest blank is better than a guess, because the copy may only use facts that end up in the ledger.

## Required fields

| Field | What to write | Why it is required |
|---|---|---|
| offer | What you sell, in one sentence | The ad has one idea. This is its subject |
| price and its basis | The number, per what, what it includes, what it excludes | A price without a basis is a policy risk and a trust problem |
| dates | Departures, season or booking window. Weekdays included | Dates are checked against a calendar |
| audience segment | Who they are, what they already do, where they are | Decides the first line |
| platform and placement | Meta feed, Reels or Stories, X post, X card | Decides slots and budgets |
| verified proof | Real proof and where it was verified, or "none" | The skill will not invent proof |

## Ask if missing (the ads get much better with these)

| Field | What to write |
|---|---|
| landing page | The URL and the price it shows |
| cancellation and weather policy | The exact rule, in your words |
| honest limit | Who this is not for, or what does not work |
| real guest words | A review or message you have permission to quote, with the source. Leave blank if none |
| call to action | What the next step is: see dates, check availability, book |

## Optional fields

| Field | What to write | Default |
|---|---|---|
| brand voice | Three adjectives plus a paragraph of real writing in your voice | Plain, specific, one person talking |
| awareness level | unaware, problem-aware, solution-aware, product-aware or most-aware | problem-aware |
| market sophistication | Stage 1 to 5. Check competitor ads in the Meta Ad Library | stage 3 |
| words to avoid | Terms the business never uses | none |
| number of angles | 2 to 4 | 3 |

## Filled example (fictional brief)

This brief is fictional. Brekke Ski Guides does not exist. The prices, dates and numbers are made up. The same brief drives the examples in assets/output-meta-static.md and assets/output-x-post.md.

```text
offer: A guided introduction to ski touring, one day, called "Intro to ski touring".
price and basis:
  price: NOK 4,900
  per: person, for the day
  includes: guide, loan of a transceiver, probe and shovel, route planning
  excludes: skis, skins and boots (hire is NOK 650 extra), lunch, travel to the meeting point
dates: Saturdays, 6 Feb to 27 Mar 2027. That is eight Saturdays (6, 13, 20 and 27 Feb;
  6, 13, 20 and 27 Mar). Booking closes at 18:00 on the Thursday before.
audience segment: Confident resort skiers in southern Norway who have never toured.
  They ski red runs, own a resort pass and are tired of lift queues on weekends.
platform and placement: Meta feed (Facebook and Instagram), single image, 4:5.
  Also one X post with a website card.
verified proof (and where verified): None as a review. Checkable facts in the booking
  terms: group cap of six per guide, refund rule below. Guide certification: owner says
  yes, no certificate number supplied yet.
landing page and the price it shows: brekkeskiguides.example/intro shows NOK 4,900
  per person and the eight dates.
cancellation and weather policy: Free cancellation up to 7 days before. If the guide
  calls the day off for avalanche risk, the guest picks another Saturday or gets a full refund.
honest limit: Not for people who have not skied before. Guests must ski red runs with control.
real guest words (with source): none
call to action: Check dates and book.
brand voice: Plain, dry, a bit understated. Written by a guide, not a marketing team.
awareness level: problem-aware
market sophistication: stage 3 (guesses, not checked against competitor ads)
words to avoid: none given
number of angles: 3
typical day (from the owner): meet 08:30, skins on 09:15, first descent about 11:40,
  back at the car about 15:30. Conditions can shift it.
teaches on the day: putting on skins, kick turns, using the transceiver.
```

What adquill does with this brief:

- Ledger: every line becomes a row. "Guide certification" stays at verified no and is not allowed in copy until a certificate number arrives.
- Proof: there are no reviews, so the ads use the group cap, the refund rule and the schedule as proof, and the receipts say so.
- Diagnosis: problem-aware, stage 3, job is "do something different on a weekend that I can already almost do", main anxiety is "am I good enough".
- Angles: the typical day (concreteness), the refund rule (risk reversal), the level requirement (honest constraint).
